# digest-paper — Research papers

Type-specific content emphasis for the shared ingest protocol.

- Distinguish the research question, method, assumptions, dataset or apparatus, results and limitations.
- Keep measured results separate from simulations, hypotheses and comparisons reported from other papers.
- Attach experimental conditions, baselines, units and evidence anchors to quantitative claims.
- Capture reproducibility details only when supplied: code, data, setup and stated constraints.
