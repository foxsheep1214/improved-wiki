# digest-standard — Standards

Type-specific content emphasis for the shared ingest protocol.

- Identify the issuing body, edition, amendments and stated scope.
- Keep normative requirements separate from recommendations, examples and informative annexes.
- Preserve clause numbers, exact thresholds, units, exceptions and applicability conditions.
- Record supersession or effective dates only when evidenced; do not infer current legal applicability from the document alone.
