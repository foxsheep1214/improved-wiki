# digest-book — Books

Type-specific content emphasis for the shared ingest protocol.

- Preserve the argument across chapters: definitions, assumptions, core claims and their evidence.
- Track materially important entities, concepts, equations and cross-chapter dependencies; passing mentions remain context.
- Use chapter/section/page anchors for reusable claims and distinguish the author’s position from cited views.
- Keep the source summary concise and selective; link pages that materially explain the book.
