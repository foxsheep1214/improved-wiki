# digest-designexample — Design examples

Type-specific content emphasis for the shared ingest protocol.

- Preserve requirements, topology, device choices, key calculations and validation conditions.
- Explain why a choice works within the example’s constraints; do not present example values as universal rules.
- Keep simulations, measured evidence and design targets distinct, with figure/table/section anchors.
- Capture transferable design methods and limitations; incidental BOM parts need no standalone pages.
