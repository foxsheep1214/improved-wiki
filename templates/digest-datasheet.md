# digest-datasheet — Component datasheets

Type-specific content emphasis for the shared ingest protocol.

- Distinguish absolute maximum ratings from recommended operating conditions and guaranteed specifications.
- Preserve min/typ/max, units, revision, package and test conditions; transcribe equations and structured data exactly.
- Anchor numerical claims to the relevant table, figure, section or page. Preserve conflicts instead of assuming a table always overrides prose.
- Capture materially relevant pin functions, register fields, timing and application constraints. Use tables where they clarify the relevant page.
- Focus on the described component and genuinely reusable concepts. Referenced part numbers and manufacturers do not automatically warrant pages.
